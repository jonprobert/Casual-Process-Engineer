# AFT Arrow

AFT Arrow is a fluid dynamic simulation tool used to calculate pressure drop and flow distribution in gas piping and ducting systems. It can be used to accurately simulate individual components and their interaction including effects of heat transfer, real gases and choked flow.

For liquid (and low-velocity gas) containing systems, use [[AFT Fathom]].

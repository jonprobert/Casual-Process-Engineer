# Critical pressure ratio

The critical pressure ratio is defined as:

$$\frac{P_{c}}{P_u}=\left (\frac{2}{k+1}  \right )^{\left ( \frac{k}{k-1} \right )}$$

Where:

$P_c$ is the critical pressure (bara)
$P_u$ is the upstream pressure (bara)
$k$ is the ratio of specific heats for an ideal gas

[[Choked flow|Choking]] will occur when downstream pressure  $\leqslant P_c$.

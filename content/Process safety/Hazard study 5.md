# Hazard study 5

Hazard study 5 is commonly completed after water trials, but before any hazardous chemicals are introduced into the process. HS5 is usually a checklist-based approach. It ensures that actions from the earlier hazard studies have been completed and that any issues found during [[Hazard study 4]] and the water trials have been addressed, and the plant is ready for use with chemicals.
